    HOW IT WORKS

    Polymarket US hides other traders, but global Polymarket runs the same BTC 15-minute and hourly Up/Down windows on-chain. SHADOW scores every wallet on those windows after they settle and trusts only wallets with a long, profitable, recent record that don't trade both sides. Recent windows count more than old ones.

    Votes. During a window every smart wallet's buy of $2+ is a vote for that side. A vote counts more when the wallet's recent win rate is high and when its late 70¢+ bets (the same kind of bet SHADOW makes) have beaten the price it paid. A wallet whose late bets lose has no vote.

    Entering. In the last 3:30, SHADOW bets when 2+ smart wallets back a side with 65%+ of the vote weight, that side costs 72–95¢ on Polymarket US (it aims for 72–85¢: above 85¢ it waits for a better price and only pays up to 95¢ in the last 1:15, one size smaller), and the price isn't above fair value. Fair value averages two estimates: the chance BTC finishes on our side of the window's start price given the time left and recent volatility, and the latest global Polymarket price. Only a fresh global price can block a bet; when the BTC math alone says the price is too high, SHADOW bets the small size instead.

    Primary wallet. When the primary wallet (0x424e…263b) buys in a window, SHADOW follows its side (whichever side it put more money on) instead of waiting for the vote, with the same time window, price band and brakes. It bets strong when the other smart wallets agree, weak when they lean the other way, normal otherwise. When the primary wallet isn't trading, the vote decides as usual.

    Bet size. $5 / $10 / $15 for a weak / normal / strong signal. The size follows how strongly the top wallets agree: strong is 5+ wallets with 90%+ of the vote weight, weak is fewer than 3 wallets or under 75%, normal is everything in between.

    Managing. Every 5 seconds it checks the US price. If our side dips to 53–63¢ it adds twice the bet once ($20 on a $10 bet); if our side falls to 22¢ it sells everything. After a stop it records whether holding would have won.

    Brakes. No new bets for the rest of the UTC day after -$40, and a 30-minute pause after 2 losses in a row.

    Side by side. The plain 72¢ and 80¢ rules (one $20+ smart buy, no fair-value check, no brakes), SHADOW without DCA, SHADOW with a 15¢ stop or no stop, and SHADOW that also bets on a single smart wallet with 10+ late bets and 90%+ of them won, all trade the same windows on paper, each with its own record. "Return" compares them fairly when bet sizes differ.

    Wallets active 24h counts wallets that traded a scored window in the last day. SHADOW remembers up to 3,000 wallets (the longest records), so that total stays at 3,000 while the active count moves. No real money is used.
