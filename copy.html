    HOW IT WORKS

    Polymarket US hides other traders, but global Polymarket runs the same BTC 15-minute and hourly Up/Down windows on-chain. SHADOW scores every wallet on those windows after they settle, and trusts only wallets with a long, profitable, recent record that don't trade both sides.

    When a trusted wallet buys Up or Down, SHADOW makes the same $10 paper bet on the matching Polymarket US window at the US price plus the taker fee, one per window. It skips when the US price is much worse than theirs, above 95¢, or too close to the close. No real money is used.
