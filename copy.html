    HOW IT WORKS

    Wallets active 24h counts wallets that traded a scored window in the last day. SHADOW remembers up to 3,000 wallets (the ones with the longest records); past that it drops the least useful, so the total stays at 3,000 while the active count moves.

    Polymarket US hides other traders, but global Polymarket runs the same BTC 15-minute and hourly Up/Down windows on-chain. SHADOW scores every wallet on those windows after they settle, and trusts only wallets with a long, profitable, recent record that don't trade both sides.

    During each window SHADOW adds up what trusted wallets buy on each side. In the last 4 minutes, if they backed a side and that side costs 72–95¢ on Polymarket US, it makes a $10 paper bet on it (US price plus the taker fee), one per window.

    While it holds a copy, it checks the US price every 5 seconds. If our side dips to 53–63¢ it adds $20 once; if our side falls to 22¢ it sells everything. After a stop it records whether holding would have won, so you can see if the stop is helping. No real money is used.
